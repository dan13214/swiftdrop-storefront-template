What we collect, and why

When you place an order

  • Your name and email address, and your delivery address.
  • What you bought, what you paid and when.

We use these to take payment, have your order made and delivered, give you your download, send you order messages and deal with any problem. Our legal reason is that we need them to carry out our contract with you.

We also keep a record of each sale because tax law requires it. Our legal reason there is a legal obligation.

Your card details go straight to Stripe. We never see or store them.

When you email us

We keep your email address and your message so we can reply and keep track of what was agreed. Our legal reason is our legitimate interest in answering customers.

When you visit the site

We count visits using Vercel Web Analytics. It does not use cookies and does not identify you. It tells us things like which pages are read and what kind of device was used. Our legal reason is our legitimate interest in knowing whether the site works.

Like any website, our host briefly records technical details such as your IP address to deliver pages and stop abuse. If something breaks at checkout, an error report is sent to our error-monitoring service so we can fix it.

The free tools run in your browser. What you type into them is not sent to us.The day-rate calculator on the sales page runs in your browser. The figures you type into it are not sent to us.

Cookies

We do not set any cookies of our own. When the checkout loads, Stripe sets cookies that it needs to take payment securely and prevent fraud. The site's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google to fetch them.

Who we share your details with

Only the companies we need to run the shop:

  • Stripe: takes your payment.
  • Printful: prints your order and arranges delivery, so it receives your name and delivery address. The delivery company it uses receives them too.
  • Vercel: hosts this website and provides the visit counts.
  • Railway: hosts the system that stores orders.
  • Resend: sends order emails.
  • Sentry: receives error reports.

We may also have to share information with HMRC, or with the police or a court, if the law requires it. We never sell your details or pass them on for advertising.

Some of these companies store or handle data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, they rely on safeguards that UK data protection law recognises, such as the UK-US data bridge or standard contract terms approved for international transfers.

How long we keep it

  • Order records: 6 years after the end of the tax year of your order, then deleted.
  • Emails: up to 2 years after our last message to each other.
