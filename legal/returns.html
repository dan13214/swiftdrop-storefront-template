Changing your mind

You can cancel an order for any reason up to 14 days after the day you receive it. If your order comes in more than one parcel, the 14 days start when the last parcel arrives.

How to cancel

Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL_LINK}} with your order number and say you want to cancel. You can use the form at the bottom of this page, but you do not have to. Any clear message is enough.

Sending it back

Post the item back within 14 days of telling us. We will send you the return address when you cancel. You pay the return postage. Ask the Post Office for proof of postage and keep it.

Your refund

We refund the price of the item and the standard delivery charge you paid. We pay it to the card you used, within 14 days of getting the item back or of you sending us proof that you posted it, whichever comes first.

You can try an item on to check it, as you would in a shop. If it comes back worn, washed or damaged, we may reduce the refund to reflect that.

Exchanges

We do not swap sizes directly. Return the item for a refund and place a new order for the size you want.

Faulty, damaged or wrong items

Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL_LINK}} with your order number and a photo of the problem.

  • In the first 30 days you can choose a full refund or a replacement.
  • After 30 days we will replace the item. If that does not fix it, you can have a refund or keep the item at a reduced price.

We pay any return postage for a faulty or wrong item. Often a photo is enough and you will not need to send it back.

A print that fades or peels sooner than it reasonably should counts as a fault, as long as you followed the care label.

Orders that do not arrive

If your order has not arrived 15 working days after you ordered, email us. We will track it down, send it again or refund you. If it has still not arrived 30 days after you ordered, you can cancel for a full refund.

If the file is faulty

The toolkit must be as described on the sales page, of satisfactory quality and fit for its purpose. If it is not, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL_LINK}} with your order number and tell us what is wrong and which spreadsheet program you are using.

We will fix the file or send you a working copy. If we cannot do that within a reasonable time, we will refund you. The refund goes to the card you used, within 14 days of us agreeing it.

If you cannot download it

The download button on the confirmation screen works for 24 hours. If you missed it, the link has stopped working or you have lost the file, email us your order number and we will send a new link.

If you paid and never received a working download, and we cannot get one to you, we will refund you in full.

Changing your mind

For most online purchases you have 14 days to cancel. For a digital download, that right ends when the download starts, as long as you asked for it straight away and accepted that you would lose the right. You do both by ticking the box at checkout, and we cannot start your download without it.

So once your download has started, we do not give refunds for a change of mind.
