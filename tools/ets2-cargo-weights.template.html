Every ETS2 cargo, heaviest to lightest

All 378 current Euro Truck Simulator 2 cargo types, which are the same cargos you get on TruckersMP. Weights are in tonnes for a standard single trailer. Where a cargo comes in a range, it is ranked by the top of that range.

    The blue part of each bar is the heaviest load you will see and the grey part is the lightest. Doubles, B-doubles, HCT and the DLC-only trailers can carry more or less than these figures.

    "Base game" means it is in vanilla ETS2. Anything else needs that map or cargo DLC, and on TruckersMP you only get DLC cargo if you own the DLC. "Patch 1.xx" cargos came free in that update. Cargos marked "not listed" do not have a confirmed weight yet. Loaders were removed in 1.54, so they are left out.

    Data: The Truck Simulator Wiki, ETS2 cargo types, pulled October 2026.
