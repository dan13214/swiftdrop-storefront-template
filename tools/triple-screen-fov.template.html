Triple-screen FOV calculator

A correct field of view makes the world on screen the same size it would be through a real windscreen, so speed and distance look right. It depends on only two things: how big your screen is and how far your eyes are from it.

Sims do not all ask for the same number. Assetto Corsa's FOV setting is the vertical figure. Many others ask for the horizontal figure, and some triple-screen setup screens ask for monitor width, bezel, distance and side-screen angle instead, which are all listed above. Check which one your sim wants before typing it in.

The side-screen angle is the one that points each side screen straight at your eyes, so nothing looks stretched. If the correct FOV feels too narrow on a single screen, moving the screen closer does more than raising the number.

How it is worked out: picture width comes from the diagonal and the aspect ratio. One screen's horizontal FOV is 2 × atan(width ÷ (2 × distance)), and vertical FOV is the same sum using the picture height. With three screens the bezels are added to the width, and the total is three times one screen.
